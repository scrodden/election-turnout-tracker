Registration vs. Turnout

Each party's share of ballots cast vs. its share of registered voters — who's over/under-performing

← Turnout · National · Results · About

By state

“Reg” = share of registered voters by party. “Cast” = share of ballots cast so far by registered party. “Gap” = cast share − registration share (positive = that party is casting a larger share of ballots than its registration; i.e., over-performing its registration in turnout). Only party-registration states with both figures are compared.

Registration from each state's official statistics; early-vote composition is registered party of ballots cast (not vote choice). Independent, non-partisan.
