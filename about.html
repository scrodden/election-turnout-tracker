About & Methodology

How this tracker works, what it measures, and where the data comes from

← Turnout · Results · Status

What this is

An independent, non-partisan project tracking the partisan composition of election turnout during the 2026 U.S. elections, plus live results for the U.S. House, Senate, and Governor races. It runs as a static site that refreshes from official sources every ~10 minutes (every ~2 minutes on Election Day for results).

What “partisan composition of turnout” means

For states with party registration, we show the registered party of voters who have already cast a ballot (by mail, early in person, or on Election Day) — not how they voted. A county shown as “R+10” means registered Republicans make up 10 points more of the ballots cast there than registered Democrats. It says nothing about which candidate those voters chose.

States without party registration (e.g., Georgia, Michigan, Wisconsin, Texas) are shown in turnout-only mode: total ballots cast and turnout %, with no partisan breakdown.

Voting methods

Where the source distinguishes them, turnout is broken out by mail, early in person, Election Day, and provisional ballots, plus mail ballots outstanding (sent but not yet returned) where available.

How it updates & freezes

Each state has its own connector that reads the official feed and writes a standard data file; a scheduler refreshes them. States are added as their 2026 feeds go live through October. When a state’s results are certified, its data is archived (a permanent final snapshot) and updates stop — see the feed-status page for what’s live vs. pending.

Comparisons

Florida includes a 2022 vs 2026 partisan-lean comparison. After the election, a turnout-vs-result comparison overlays each county’s partisan turnout against the actual Governor/Senate result margin, to show which counties over- or under-performed their registration.

Results & balance of power

The results page shows a national map and scorecard for the Senate, Governor, and House, colored by the current margin, with a live balance-of-power bar (seats needed for control, including the seats not up in 2026). Results are wired from official state election-night feeds.

Disclaimers

  • Party registration is not a vote choice or a projection; independents/unaffiliated voters are shown separately where reported.
  • Figures are unofficial and preliminary until certified; feeds can revise or lag.
  • Some states report by municipality or precinct and are aggregated to counties; a few use estimates where noted.
  • This is not affiliated with any state, campaign, or party.

Independent, non-partisan data project. Built on official, publicly available election data.
